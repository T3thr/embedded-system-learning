4. **GROUND-TRUTH TECHNICAL FIDELITY:**
   - 8051 classic 12T @ 12.000 MHz (1 machine cycle = 1 µs).
   - Port behaviors: P2 latch = 0FFH for inputs; P0 needs pull-ups; P1 controls motor and signals.
   - Dual-firmware distinction: Classroom edition (polling Delay 20 ms, 20 cases) vs. Extended edition (Timer 0 10 ms ISR, 36 cases).
   - Electrical isolation: 5 V logic vs. 12 V motor/sensor rails via VO617A optocouplers and 27 Ω 10 W series current-limiting resistor.
